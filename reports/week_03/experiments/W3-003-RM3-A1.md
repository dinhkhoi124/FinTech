## RM2 representativeness

RM2 covered bounded state/domain recovery, single CHECKS objectives, exact
fallback quotes, rank-2 recovery, cross-state/cross-domain distractors, and
corrective partial cover. It did not systematically cover composite/unknown
states, multiple requested dimensions, response-level obligation completeness,
multi-evidence STANDARD coverage, or route authorization crossed with bounded
discovery. Thus the prior suite could pass while a larger behavioral cross-
product remained untested. This is a concrete axis gap, not criticism of case
count alone.
