## Senior review correction

### Reproduce → Root cause

The original verifier accepted fabricated citation metadata when `evidence_id`
matched. Claim arrays were incompletely typed/aligned. Raw ANSWER counted as a
positive success without checking frozen gold/acceptable roles. Abstentions were
included as citation-correct, creating a vacuous 1.00, while unsupported claims
used cases as denominator. Extractive weights were hard-coded.

### Fix

Bound every citation field to selected evidence, hardened claim IDs and aligned
arrays, required relevant evidence (and all strict gold for multi-document),
introduced answer/claim-level nullable metrics, and wired validated config weights
into the generator. Recomputed all 12 policies; `S0.40_C0.45` remained selected.

### Regression tests → Lesson

Added metadata, alignment, relevance, multi-document, vacuous-metric, claim-level,
configured-weight, and tracked-drift mutations. Grounding evidence must bind
identity, metadata, relevance, and denominators; syntactic citation presence or
an all-abstain run cannot establish 100% citation correctness.
